# Affinisaur v0.8.1

## Fixes and refinements

- Fixed displaced and clipped controls after changing window scale, including the type selector, headers, matchup panels and team cards. The fix covers normal, pinned and locked windows.
- Locked overlay controls now fade independently. Hovering a button reveals only that button; dragging, holding and keyboard focus keep the active control visible.
- The padlock remains fully visible at all times and has clear hover feedback.
- Added regression checks for native control positions across 50–150% scaling and all four team-panel positions.

Your team, preferences and tutorial progress are preserved. Download **Affinisaur.exe** and replace your previous executable while the app is closed.
