## Affinisaur v0.8.0

Inspect suggestions before applying them, preview your display and learn with animated controls.

### Highlights

- Interactive suggestion bubbles with direct Apply and radar comparison.
- Complete, scrollable matchup bubbles and illustrated suggestion help.
- Theme and glass hover previews, with more readable glass labels.
- Animated tutorials, Play all / unfinished and a fresh skippable returning-user tour.
- Consistent keyboard-key badges in tooltips and tutorials.
- Less work and allocation while scrolling long matchup lists.

### Suggestions you can explore

- Interactive suggestion bubbles stay open while hovered and offer Radar preview, Apply suggestion, an open-window button and a close button. Ordinary tooltips retain their timed cooldown indicator.
- Browse all proposals in a persistent tabbed window, with the opened suggestion selected and element icons retained.
- Compare a suggested ally with your team on radar charts, highlight individual traces by hovering the legend, and apply directly from the preview.
- Hover checked ally cards to emphasize their traces on the main radars. Remaining suggestions recalculate after an application; earlier unapplied proposals still count as projections, not committed allies.

- Matchup lists now open as hoverable, scrollable bubbles with all entries and a close button, instead of truncating after six cards.
- Suggestion help uses compact illustrated sections and keyboard-key badges for shortcuts.

### Display previews and glass

- Compact Display cards combine theme, text, scale, badges and independent glass options for locked overlays, secondary windows and the main window.
- Theme and glass hover previews are temporary and restore when leaving. Apply saves the choices; the appearance icon updates accordingly. Main-window glass previews target the parent window.
- Interactive glass uses a slightly stronger translucent veil and higher-contrast secondary labels. Shared scrolling, borders and corners have been refined.

### Learn at your own pace

- Pin/lock, suggestions and radar tutorials animate real controls using isolated demo state, without changing your team or settings.
- Click tutorial progress points to jump between topics. Completed points stay checked; skipping ahead does not complete intermediate topics.
- Help has a compact header and tutorial rows, Play all, Play unfinished and a Resume action when a tour is paused.
- Returning users receive a fresh, skippable three-step v0.8 tour, including users who already saw v0.7 or hid introductory tutorials. Other completion history is preserved.
- Tutorials follow Display settings. Update prompts now include Remind me later alongside Skip this version.

### Rendering and performance

- Shortcut keycaps are shared across ordinary tooltips, tutorial explanations and Pause/Skip controls; configured shortcuts keep their current bindings.
- Long matchup bubbles only draw visible rows. In a local 45-entry probe, median clipped paint time fell from 1.004 to 0.676 ms and allocations from 11.0 to 1.5 MB over 120 draws.
- Glass positioning avoids duplicate notifications and reuses positioning arrays. Movement timings remained essentially unchanged; no drag frame-rate gain is claimed.

### Compatibility and validation

Existing preference locations and update paths are retained. Release packaging provides Affinisaur.exe, the identical AniimoAffinities.exe compatibility asset and SHA256SUMS.txt.

Suggestions use elemental matchups, not species, moves or combat statistics. This build remains unsigned. Windows 10 visual acceptance, mixed-DPI hardware, screen-reader and capture-software testing remain outstanding; automated checks do not replace these manual checks.

Formatting, analyzer/compiler checks, storage/model/UI regression groups and publication-script tests pass locally. The release workflow rebuilds and tests the publication commit before uploading assets. Native Windows 10, mixed-DPI and capture-software checks remain as listed above.
