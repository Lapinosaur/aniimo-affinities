# Affinisaur v0.9.1

## Smoother tutorials

- Enter consistently advances to the next step, even when another navigation button has focus. Queued input is blocked while invisible demonstration windows are being prepared.
- Skip the current tutorial to move to the next one, or skip the entire tour. Skipped tutorials are not marked completed; Pause remains available separately.
- Escape now behaves like the Skip button in the What's new tour, preserving completion history and preventing automatic reopening.
- Tutorial settings previews no longer change the real theme on hover. Animated scenes refresh when the theme changes.

## Cleaner themes and transitions

- External links show a tooltip with their destination and explain that they open in your browser, including support links and QR buttons.
- Theme cards wait 450 ms before previewing. The preview stays active while hovered and for 450 ms after leaving, then restores the applied theme. Preferences are saved only with Apply.
- Fixed stale light/dark backgrounds around support buttons and threat-report controls.
- Fixed stretched icons and squeezed cards during team-panel transitions by capturing the full destination layout.

## More reliable updates

- GitHub release asset names are read independently of the interface language.
- The update lock uses the same technical filename in all 30 languages, preventing separate update operations from bypassing each other.
- Future releases provide one executable, **Affinisaur.exe**, plus **SHA256SUMS.txt**. Automatic Discord announcements hide the former executable name. Historical download links remain available.

Your team, settings and tutorial history are preserved. This patch does not automatically replay the v0.9.0 news tour.

If v0.9.0 cannot detect this update in your language, temporarily switch the interface to English and check again, or close the app and replace its executable with Affinisaur.exe from this release. Your local preferences are stored separately.
