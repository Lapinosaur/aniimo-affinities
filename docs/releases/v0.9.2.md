# Affinisaur v0.9.2

## Clear background in locked mode

- The main background stays fully transparent while the overlay is locked, even when main-window frosted glass is enabled.
- Pin changes and transparency previews respect the locked state instead of reactivating glass across the entire window.
- Panel glass still follows the Locked overlay preference. Unlocking restores the normal main-window glass preference.

Your team, settings and tutorial history are preserved. This patch does not replay the news tour.
