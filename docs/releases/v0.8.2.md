# Affinisaur v0.8.2

## Support made easier

- Open the support bubble to scan the QR code with your phone or click it directly to visit Buy me a carrot. A phone icon, clearer instructions and hover feedback make both options easy to find.
- Use the real **Star on GitHub** button in the bubble or in Settings > Help & Tutorial > Support. GitHub opens in your browser; sign in and click Star to support the project for free.
- Release announcements now include a direct invitation to star the repository.

## Interface refinements

- The Support page fits its content instead of leaving a large empty area. Switching back to Tutorials restores the list height, with a smooth transition and scrolling on smaller screens.
- Display preview cards share consistent selection, hover and keyboard-focus styling.
- Interactive bubbles can be dismissed with Escape; close icons use the shared vector style.
- The app and Settings identify Affinisaur as an unofficial Aniimo companion.
- Improved the Lock tutorial animation timer and its regression checks.

Your team, preferences and tutorial progress are preserved. Close Affinisaur before replacing the executable.

This release distributes the portable Windows executable. Experimental MSIX preparation is available in the source repository for developers; no Microsoft Store edition is published.
